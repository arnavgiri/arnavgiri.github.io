The method / 02

How the age calculator works

Age is counted using complete calendar years and months, followed by any remaining days. For example, someone born on 15 June 2000 is 26 years, 2 months and 17 days old on 1 September 2026.

The calculator advances by whole calendar months from the birth date. If the matching day doesn't exist in a shorter month, it uses that month's final day. It then counts the remaining calendar days, rather than assuming every month contains 30 days or every year contains 365 days.

Important to know

For a person born on 29 February, this calculator treats 28 February as the birthday in non-leap years. Some official rules use 1 March instead; always follow the rule in the relevant application or jurisdiction.

Questions / 04

Age calculator FAQs

Clear answers to common questions about dates, birthdays and age calculations.

How do I calculate my exact age from my date of birth?

Enter your date of birth and choose the date you want to calculate your age on. This calculator counts complete calendar years, then completed months, then remaining days. The result appears immediately.

Can I calculate my age as on a past or future date?

Yes. Change the “Calculate age on” field to any valid date on or after your birthday. This is helpful for school admissions, exams and other eligibility cut-offs.

How many days old am I?

The “Days lived” result counts the actual number of calendar days from your birthday to the selected date, including leap days. It does not approximate using 365 days per year.

What happens if I was born on 29 February?

Leap-day birthdays are valid. For non-leap years, this calculator uses 28 February for anniversary and birthday calculations. Legal and organizational rules can vary, so confirm the applicable convention.

Can I use this for an exam age limit?

You can use it to calculate completed age on an announced cut-off date. However, eligibility may include separate relaxation rules, birth-date windows and document requirements. Read the official notification before applying.

Does the calculator store my birthday?

No. The calculation runs in your browser. Your input is not sent to a server or saved in local storage by this tool.
